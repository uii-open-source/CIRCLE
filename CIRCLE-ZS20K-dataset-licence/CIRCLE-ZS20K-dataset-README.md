# CIRCLE-ZS20K

Version 20261110

## What is the CIRCLE-ZS20K dataset

CIRCLE-ZS20K ([https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhongshan-rad-fudan/CIRCLE-ZS20K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhongshan-rad-fudan/CIRCLE-ZS20K)) contains 20,000 de-identified chest CT examinations paired with radiology reports and abnormality labels extracted from the reports. For more details, please see our [official GitHub website](https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE) (https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE).

## Why we are releasing it and how you can use it

Sincerely, we encourage academic colleagues to use CIRCLE-ZS20K to test new hypotheses, revisit our assumptions and pursue questions beyond the original CIRCLE study. By sharing this resource, we hope to support reproducible research and contribute to an academic community in which knowledge grows through open exchange. Research topics are unrestricted, and we impose no requirement for co-authorship with our team or for pre-submission review of manuscripts.

Access to CIRCLE-ZS20K is restricted to non-commercial academic use. This restriction reflects regulatory requirements rather than a preference of the authors: the scans and reports were contributed by a hospital under its institutional data-governance rules, and their release is further governed by the laws and regulations of the country in which the data were collected and from which they are distributed. We are therefore unable to grant unrestricted or commercial access, and each approved project must operate under the published License and a signed Data Use Agreement (DUA), which together prohibit re-identification, redistribution and any commercial application. Eligibility is assessed on a per-project basis, and we will consider requests from non-commercial academic groups whose intended use falls within these terms.

## How to request access

a) **Initial enquiry from the principal investigator.** Download the **access query form** (.xlsx) from our official GitHub repository (https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE), complete the shaded entry cells and checklist, and email it directly to **[circle_collaborators@zs-hospital.sh.cn](mailto:circle_collaborators@zs-hospital.sh.cn)**. **Please submit this form before requesting access on Hugging Face**. It must be completed and sent by the principal investigator, using their own **INSTITUTIONAL** email address, which will serve as the verified primary contact for the DUA and all project correspondence. This gives us a reliable institutional point of contact for responsible data sharing.

b) **Preliminary review.** We normally send a decision within **15 working days** of receiving the completed access query form; during periods of high demand, this stage may take up to 45 working days. Applicants whose enquiry is approved receive a preliminary approval reference number and the DUA, and may then submit their request on Hugging Face. Please quote that reference number in the request form.

c) **Signed DUA and final review**. The DUA includes the full License. Please read it in full, enter the principal investigator's and institution's details, sign the final acceptance page, and return the **COMPLETE** signed DUA from the same principal investigator email address used in step a. The final review period starts only once we have both a fully signed DUA and a matching pending request on Hugging Face. We normally complete this review **within 15 working days**, or up to 45 working days during periods of high demand, and approve eligible accounts once the DUA is accepted.

Working days follow the Provider's institutional working calendar in Shanghai, China; time awaiting missing or corrected applicant material or signatures is excluded. An email, submitted form, preliminary result or signed DUA alone does not grant access. If a response is overdue, please contact **zeng.mengsu@zs-hospital.sh.cn** with the application reference and original thread.

Application materials may undergo automated or AI-assisted checks, followed by human preliminary review. Final access authorization is confirmed by a human reviewer. Please submit the original editable workbook for pre-contact and a complete, legible signed DUA when requested. Application material is handled for access administration and related institutional records. Please omit patient data, passwords, tokens and unrelated confidential material, and send application documents by email rather than posting them publicly.

## What documents are required, and how do we share data responsibly?

The **access query form** is the only pre-contact guarantee form required. Its declaration and all fields are contained in the [access query form workbook](https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE/blob/main/CIRCLE-ZS20K-dataset-licence/CIRCLE_ZS20K_access_query_form.xlsx). Reference copies of the [Licence](https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE/blob/main/CIRCLE-ZS20K-dataset-licence/Licence.pdf) and the [DUA](https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE/blob/main/CIRCLE-ZS20K-dataset-licence/DUA.pdf) are provided in the `CIRCLE-ZS20K-dataset-licence` directory of our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/uii-open-source/CIRCLE); they are for information only and are not signing texts. After preliminary review, the Provider issues the DUA carrying the applicant's preliminary approval reference. That issued DUA, which incorporates the full Licence text, is the only document to be signed and returned, and the version it contains prevails.

Medical data sharing remains subject to hospital oversight and applicable regulations. We may adjust review arrangements as those requirements evolve and will announce material changes and their effective dates on this page. We welcome responsible academic use and aim to make access practical for academic colleagues.

Protecting patient privacy is our highest priority. We have therefore applied well-established technical methods to de-identify all patient information contained in CIRCLE-ZS20K, and the dataset has passed testing by an independent third-party organization; this is a prerequisite for its safe release. However, if you notice any content that could identify an individual, please report it privately to [circle_collaborators@zs-hospital.sh.cn](mailto:circle_collaborators@zs-hospital.sh.cn) and avoid posting the material publicly. We will take immediate action to correct or remove the relevant content and update the dataset accordingly.
